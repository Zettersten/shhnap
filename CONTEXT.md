# Shnapp
The vocabulary of a fast, local screenshot-and-annotation utility.
## Language
**Shnapp**: The Windows screenshot application and product.
**shnapp**: A captured piece of the screen, together with its editable annotations.
_Avoid_: snip, screenshot (except where needed for discoverability).
**shnapping**: The act of taking a shnapp.
**window shnapp**: A capture of one selected application window.
**full-screen shnapp**: A capture of the display containing the pointer.
**free-form shnapp**: A user-selected region of the screen. The initial version uses a rectangle.
**step**: A numbered circular annotation identifying a point in a sequence.
**redaction**: An opaque annotation that permanently replaces covered pixels in a shared or exported shnapp.
**library**: The user's saved shnapps.
